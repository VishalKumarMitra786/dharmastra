# Escape from Hastinapur (Dvapara Yuga)

## Tradition followed
Setting beat: the tense weeks of Udyoga Parva, the book of preparation, when envoys travel to Hastinapura and both sides gather allies before the Kurukshetra war. We follow the general Sanskrit-epic tradition as summarized in the critical-edition outline. We do not depict any epic figure on screen. They are only referred to.

## Logline
On a tense night before the great war, a young chariot driver must carry a villagers' plea for grain relief through Hastinapur's lantern-lit streets and out the east gate before dawn.

No date for the war itself is given. Proposed dates are contested and we do not present any as settled.

## Characters
- **Tarak** (protagonist, mortal). A young chariot driver from the royal stables.
  - Want: keep the east road open for the villages he grew up near.
  - Flaw: he trusts his own speed over a plan, and he goes quiet when afraid.
  - Voice: short, dry, practical.
- **Suvrata**. A courier who writes for the farm villages and carries their plea.
  - Want: get the sealed plea to the village elders by morning.
  - Flaw: she hides how afraid she is, and she over-plans.
  - Voice: precise and calm, with sharp asides.
- **Bhadra**. Stable master and Tarak's mentor.
  - Want: Tarak safe, and the stables left alone.
  - Flaw: caution that slides into nagging.
  - Voice: gruff and warm.
- **Captain Dandaka**. Commander of the east gate watch.
  - Want: order kept in a city on edge.
  - Flaw: stiff pride in his duty.
  - Voice: formal, then plain once he decides.
- The watch are ordinary soldiers doing a hard night's duty. No group is a villain.

## Mission beats
- **Opening:** night, the stable yard. Word of an envoy's visit has doubled the patrols. The plea must leave before the dawn gate opens.
- **Objective 1, checkpoint-1:** reach the Lamp Bazaar and take on Suvrata and the sealed plea.
- **Objective 2, checkpoint-2:** cross the canal bridge before the watch bells close it.
- **Objective 3, checkpoint-3:** reach the East Gate before first light.
- **Twist:** the plea is not a secret about the war. It asks for grain relief, because fields are being requisitioned. Captain Dandaka reads it and opens the gate himself, since his own kin farm those fields.
- **Finale:** a short sprint on the east road. Dawn conches sound far off, and a light flashes on the sky.
- **Fail and retry:** a hit costs nothing and slows the cart. Three hits, or a closed bridge, ends the run. Bhadra calls Tarak back. Retry restarts from the last checkpoint with patrol routes shifted a little. Fail lines stay kind.

## Scenes

### SCENE 1: EXT. ROYAL STABLE YARD, HASTINAPUR, NIGHT
Lamps burn low. Horses shift in the dark. Tarak checks the harness.

BHADRA
The city is on edge. Keep your lamp low and your wheels quiet, Tarak.

TARAK
Three stops, then the east road. I know the way.

*Hook:* tutorial driving. Steering, braking, a lantern cone to avoid. Subtitle plus on-screen arrow to checkpoint 1.

### SCENE 2: EXT. LAMP BAZAAR, NIGHT (checkpoint 1)
Shutters are half closed. A patrol passes at the far end. Suvrata steps from a doorway with a sealed tablet.

SUVRATA
Here. The plea from the farm villages. Hold the tablet flat and drive.

TARAK
Hold tight. The canal bridge is next.

*Hook:* stealth driving. Weave around patrol lantern cones. Patrol path loops every 20 seconds so it can be learned.

### SCENE 3: EXT. CANAL BRIDGE, NIGHT (checkpoint 2)
Watch bells begin to ring. The bridge deck is about to be raised. A lamp flashes on the tower.

SUVRATA
The bells are ringing. Once the bridge lifts, we are stuck.

TARAK
Across. Now the east gate, and the dawn is coming.

*Hook:* timed chase. A visible countdown bar and a flashing tower lamp. The bells are never the only warning.

### SCENE 4: EXT. EAST GATE, BEFORE DAWN (checkpoint 3, twist)
Captain Dandaka lowers the tablet. Torches flicker. The watch waits.

CAPTAIN DANDAKA
Open the gate. I read your tablet. Those fields feed my kin too.

*Hook:* cutscene beat, then a short dash through the opened gate.

### SCENE 5: EXT. EAST ROAD, DAWN
The cart rolls into pale light. Fields stretch ahead.

SUVRATA
The plea will reach the village elders by morning. Thank you, Tarak.

TARAK
No glory in this. Just a road that stayed open.

*Hook:* wind-down drive to the finish marker.

### Fail and hit lines (any scene)
- Hit: SUVRATA, "That was a lantern cone. Stay in the shadows!" or TARAK, "The wheel is shaking. Steady, steady."
- Fail: BHADRA, "They stopped the cart. Come back to the stable and try another lane." or SUVRATA, "We can still go. The watch changes at the bells. Wait for them."

## Player choices
- At the bazaar: take the narrow back lanes (slower, fewer patrols) or the main road (faster, more patrols).
- At the bridge: cross now with a hit risk, or wait one bell for a patrol to pass.
- Both branches reach the same ending. Choice only changes difficulty and a closing line.

## Accessibility notes
- Every spoken line is captioned, with the speaker's name shown.
- No clue is carried by sound alone. Bells, conches and horns each pair with a visible flash, a bar or an icon.
- Patrol cones are shaped and outlined, not distinguished by color alone.
- Objectives are text, with a marker and a distance readout.
- Reduced-motion setting turns off screen shake and lamp flicker.
