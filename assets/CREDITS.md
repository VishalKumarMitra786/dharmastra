# Asset credits
Every file under assets/ needs a line here: source URL and licence. tools/check-budget.mjs fails without one.

## characters/ (hero spike, built by tools/build-hero.mjs)
- characters/hero.glb: derived from the MakeHuman hm08 base mesh (`makehuman/data/3dobjs/base.obj`), default skeleton (`makehuman/data/rigs/default.mhskel`) and default bone weights (`makehuman/data/rigs/default_weights.mhw`), reduced and re-rigged to 28 bones by our script, with garment shells, shoes, hair, headgear and cloth tails generated by tools/build-hero.mjs and src/gfx/human (no third-party meshes, textures or animation; all textures are painted procedurally on a canvas at run time). Source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman/master/ (repository https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman). Licence: CC0 1.0 Universal (the files say so in their headers; LICENSE.md section C grants the assets, which include the base mesh and rigs, under CC0 1.0).
- characters/hero.sources.json: our own record of the five upstream files with their sha256, used to stop the build if upstream changes. No third-party content.
- characters/makehuman-LICENSE.md: copy of https://raw.githubusercontent.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman/master/LICENSE.md (MakeHuman licence overview; assets are CC0 1.0, source code is AGPL and is not used).
- characters/makehuman-LICENSE.ASSETS.md: copy of https://raw.githubusercontent.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman/master/LICENSE.ASSETS.md (the CC0 1.0 Universal legal text).

## world/ (environment kit and ground, modelled and baked in-house with Blender)
- world/kit.glb: 16 modular pieces (arched window with louvred shutters, arched door, fluted column, market stall with goods, cart, lantern, jar, barrel, crate, bench, sack, basket, banner, rope coil, tiled hip roof, door awning), each with a detailed version baked down to a game version and a decimated LOD1. Modelled from primitives by tools/blender/world/pieces.py and tools/blender/world/build_kit.py (Blender 5.3, run headless; Blender is GPL, its output is ours). No third-party meshes or textures. Licence: original work of this project, redistributable with the game (the repo licence applies).
- world/kit_normal.jpg, world/kit_normal_1k.jpg, world/kit_orm_1k.jpg: tangent normal map and a packed map (red ambient occlusion, green curvature) baked from the detailed pieces by build_kit.py; the packed map is 1024 for every tier (occlusion and curvature are low frequency); kit_normal_1k.jpg is the half-size normal for the Low tier. Same source and licence as kit.glb.
- world/ground_cobble_albedo.jpg, world/ground_cobble_normal.jpg: 2 m tileable cobble patch, modelled as high-poly stones on a periodic lattice and baked by tools/blender/world/bake_ground.py. Same source and licence.
- Historical labels (also in the comments of pieces.py): arched and shuttered lime-plaster houses, lotus capitals, clay roof tiles, bullock carts and brass lanterns are Tradition (no single date); every specific design is Invented. Nothing here is a copy of a real monument.

## characters/anim/ (authored animation clips, built by tools/blender/anim)
- characters/anim/loco.glb, characters/anim/combat.glb, characters/anim/react.glb, characters/anim/interact.glb: animation-only glTF files (rotation tracks as normalised int16 quaternions, one hips_root translation track) for the 28-bone skeleton in characters/hero.glb. Every clip was written in code by our scripts in tools/blender/anim (key poses, a gait generator with foot planting and two-bone IK), keyed into Blender 5.3 actions through its Python module (bpy, run headless), read back from Blender, checked and written by tools/blender/anim/rig.py. No motion capture, no Mixamo, no third-party or purchased clips and no retargeted data: the only inputs are the bone names and rest offsets of our own hero.glb. Source: this repository (tools/blender/anim/*.py). Licence: original in-house work. Blender itself is GPL; the files it helped produce are ours.

## characters/high/ (High tier head pack, built in Blender 5.3 by tools/blender/characters/*.py; lazily loaded)
Everything here is original work generated by our scripts from the CC0 MakeHuman base mesh (same upstream files, same pins and licence as characters/hero.glb: https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman, CC0 1.0 Universal; see characters/makehuman-LICENSE.md). Textures are procedural (noise, k-d tree scatter, PIL strokes), no scans or third-party images. Blender is GPL, its output is ours.
- characters/high/head-high.glb: full-resolution head and neck (MakeHuman hm08 base, 8.6k triangles), eyeballs, lash and brow cards, teeth, tongue; 14 identity and 7 expression shape keys as int8 deltas. CC0-1.0 (derived) plus our own geometry.
- characters/high/hair.glb: hair and beard cards (crop, short, long, bun, topknot, braid, moustache, goatee, short and full beard) grown on the base head surface by tools/blender/characters/hairstyles.py. Original, CC0-1.0 for the head surface it follows.
- characters/high/hair_strands.webp, characters/high/cards.webp, characters/high/eye_atlas.webp, characters/high/eye_orm.webp: procedural strand, brow, lash and iris textures. Original.
- characters/high/skin_albedo_1024.webp, characters/high/skin_normal_1024.webp, characters/high/skin_orm_1024.webp: baked skin maps (pores, folds, wrinkles, stubble mask). Original.
